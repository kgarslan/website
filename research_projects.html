History, Memory, and Identification

History, Memory, and Identification: An Ethnographic Study of the Alsatian Population of Turkish Origin

Project Leader: Kerem Görkem ARSLAN

Scientific Committee:

  • Samim AKGÖNÜL, University of Strasbourg
  • Stéphane DE TAPIA, University of Strasbourg

This study aims to establish an updated research foundation on the diverse communities that shape contemporary Alsace, enrich museum collections, and contribute to the development of museographic elements for the future visitor experience at the Musée Alsacien.

Focusing on the Alsatian population of Turkish origin, our approach seeks to deepen the analysis of migration to France, as well as the experiences of generations born, educated, and socialized in Alsace within this migratory context. We explore the underlying motivations driving this phenomenon while assessing the integration process within the Alsatian community, particularly for non-migrant generations. A key aspect of our field research is the special attention given to specific dates and celebrations, viewing these moments as significant milestones for participant observation in this study.

SoLaRe (Sociétés, Laïcités, Religions) – Doctoral Laboratory of the ReligiS Programme

Co-Founder / Co-Director: Kerem Görkem Arslan

Executive Committee: Julien Argoud (Sciences Po), Mehmet Balsever (INALCO), Priscilla Bucher (AMU), Ellie Douska (Lyon III), Mathilde Dravet (EPHE), Emmanuel Falguières (Lyon 2), Tara-Lou Iftene (EHESS), Andrea Leonardi (ENS Lyon), Aryan Rahimian (Université de Lorraine)

SoLaRe is a doctoral laboratory founded by and for doctoral researchers as a scientific animation structure of the interdisciplinary ReligiS programme (Religions and Societies Facing Contemporary Challenges – France 2030). It supports high-level research training across the eighty laboratories affiliated with ReligiS, focusing on renewing methodological approaches, fostering interdisciplinarity, and internationalising doctoral work on religious phenomena.

The laboratory is steered by a collegial executive committee of ten doctoral researchers representing the consortium's institutions, renewed annually, and is organised around three thematic axes: the production, transmission, and circulation of the religious; lived religion (individual, intimate, collective, and social experiences); and the powers, institutionalisation, and administration of religious phenomena.

INOPS – Interdisciplinary Network of Pagan Studies

Founder: Kerem Görkem Arslan (July 2025)

Website: paganstudies.com

Support: ITI MAKErS and UMR 7354 DRES, University of Strasbourg

INOPS brings together researchers working on revived local faiths, contemporary Paganisms, and related spiritual, cultural, and social movements, fostering collaboration and interdisciplinary dialogue across sociology, anthropology, political science, law, history of religions, cultural studies, and folklore studies.

The network grew out of the international workshop “The Echo of Ancestors: The Reemergence and Claims of 'Ancient' Religions in Europe”, held on 3–4 April 2025 at the University of Strasbourg, whose intellectual exchange revealed the need for a structured academic platform to continue the dialogue on revived religious identities and practices across Europe and beyond.

EUREL Correspondent for Turkey

Network Director: Anne-Laure Zwilling

Correspondent since: June 2023

Host Institutions: CNRS / University of Strasbourg (UMR 7354 DRES), with GSRL (CNRS / EPHE, Paris)

EUREL (EUrope – RELigion) is an international scientific network providing accurate, regularly updated sociological and legal data on the status of religion across Europe and beyond, intended for the international scientific community, public authorities, and political forces.

As the correspondent for Turkey, Kerem Görkem Arslan prepares and updates the country's overview, historical highlights, and law-and-religion sections, including an annual country report; his 2024 report on freedom of expression and religion in Turkey directly led to an invitation to contribute an English-language book chapter on the subject.

Doctoral Member – Chaire Méditerranée, religions et sociétés (PISAI)

Chair Holder: Dominique Avon

Hosting Institution: Pontificio Istituto di Studi Arabi e d'Islamistica (PISAI), Rome

Membership: 2025–2026 academic year

Created at the initiative of the Institut français Centre Saint-Louis and hosted by PISAI from the 2025 academic year, the Chair brings together universities and research institutions from both shores of the Mediterranean to support research, training, and public debate on the relationship between religions and societies in the Mediterranean world.

As a doctoral member, Kerem Görkem Arslan takes part in the Chair's seminars and doctoral meetings, including sessions held in Rome, contributing a Turkey-focused perspective to the Chair's comparative, non-confessional approach to religion and society in the Mediterranean.

DIslaTurk – Islamic Studies and Turkology as Disciplines: Dynamics of Two Research Fields in Upper Rhine Universities

Project Leaders: Dilek Sarmis, Aylin de Tapia

Funding: GECT Eucor – European Campus (seed money)

Membership: Research team member since 2023

A Franco-German research project examining the historical and institutional dynamics of Islamic Studies and Turkology as academic disciplines across universities of the Upper Rhine region, bringing together researchers from France, Germany, and Switzerland within the Eucor – European Campus network.

Kerem Görkem Arslan has contributed to the project's research activities and to the organisation of its colloquium “Intersecting Epistemologies, Captive Epistemologies: Turkish and Islamic Studies in Question”.
